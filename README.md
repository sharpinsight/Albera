**Sticker Wiki** is a community-driven platform designed to catalog, track, and preserve the history of sticker albums from all eras and genres. From legendary football collections to vintage cartoon albums, this is the digital home for collectors.

## 🚀 Vision
Our goal is to bridge the gap between nostalgic paper albums and the digital era by providing a high-performance, SEO-friendly, and easy-to-use encyclopedia for the global collecting community.

## ✨ Core Features (Planned)
- **Comprehensive Database:** Detailed pages for every album, including year, publisher, and total sticker count.
- **Digital Checklist:** Users can mark stickers as "Owned", "Need", or "Swap".
- **Advanced Search:** Filter by year, category (Sports, Movies, Games), or publisher.
- **Community Contributions:** Users can suggest edits or upload high-quality images.
